Provenance

Who said it first?

A confession sounds convincing when the suspect knows details “only the culprit could know.” Yet in 24 of 26 DNA exonerations with a false confession, the confession still held accurate crime-scene details (Garrett, 2015). An innocent person had to learn them somewhere.

Provenance reads the whole interrogation record and shows, detail by detail, whether the suspect said it first or the investigators did, with the page to check.
