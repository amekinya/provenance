Provenance

A corroboration audit of a police interrogation

Who said it first?

A confession is treated as proof when it contains details "only the perpetrator could know." Provenance checks the record: for every key detail, did the suspect say it first, or did investigators?

What this is not: it doesn't decide guilt or whether a confession is true. It shows where each detail first appears in the record, with page citations, so counsel can check.
